      Why is insulation essential in Jazan?

      The region’s climate tests every building, every day

      Jazan is a coastal city on the Red Sea where intense sun, high humidity and seasonal rains meet. These four factors cause most building problems in the region — and each one has the right insulation solution.

      01

      Strong sun and long summers

      An exposed roof absorbs the sun’s heat all day and stores it in the concrete, then passes it to the upper floors even after sunset. The result: hot rooms, AC units that never stop, and high electricity bills.

      Solution:Polyurethane foam thermal insulation with a reflective top coat that bounces sunlight back.
      Thermal Insulation →
      02

      High coastal humidity

      Air loaded with moisture and salts seeps into the pores of the concrete, reaches the reinforcing steel and starts the rust that weakens the building from within — showing up as cracks and peeling paint.

      Solution:A continuous waterproofing layer that seals the concrete pores, plus treatment of any existing cracks.
      Waterproofing →
      03

      Sudden seasonal rains

      Heavy rain exposes any weakness in a roof within hours: ponding water, leaks at corners, expansion joints and pipe outlets, then stains on ceilings and damage to furniture and finishes.

      Solution:A complete roof insulation system with reinforced corners, treated expansion joints, and a flood test before handover.
      Joint Treatment →
      04

      Groundwater close to the surface

      Near the coast, groundwater rises close to the foundations, pressing on buried walls and wicking moisture up the walls by capillary action if they are not insulated.

      Solution:Insulating foundations and retaining walls before backfilling, with systems that withstand water pressure.

      How we work

      From the first visit to the warranty certificate

      We follow the same six stages on every project, whatever its size. We never move to a stage before confirming the one before it, and we never hand over a roof before a water flood test.

      Book an Inspection Visit →

      1. Site inspection

        Our engineer visits the site, identifies leak sources, the condition of the concrete, slopes and weak points, and photographs everything before work starts.

      2. Technical report & quotation

        We propose the right system, its materials and thicknesses, and send a clear, detailed quotation with no hidden items.

      3. Surface preparation

        Cleaning the surface and removing loose material, treating cracks and expansion joints, and adjusting slopes for drainage.

      4. Applying the insulation layers

        Installing the layers in order at the specified thicknesses, reinforcing corners and drains, and measuring thickness at several points.

      5. Water flood test

        We flood the roof with water and leave it for sufficient time, then check the ceilings below to make sure there is no leak.

      6. Handover & warranty certificate

        You receive the project with a written warranty certificate of up to 10 years and a direct number for after-sales service.
